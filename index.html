Solar Drone Logistics Simulator

Rural clinics and villages across Africa depend on medical supplies, food and emergency equipment. You pilot a solar-powered delivery drone over a vast African landscape.

Deliver parcels to village beacons, ride the solar microgrid zones to recharge, dodge trees, towers and wildlife, and survive storms and load-shedding. How far can your solar reserve take you?

Controls

  • W / Up / Space — throttle (thrust)
  • A / Left — rotate nose left
  • D / Right — rotate nose right
  • S / Down — brake (extra drag)
  • P / Esc — pause   M — mute
  • Enter — start / restart

Touch devices: on-screen buttons appear below.

Objective

  • ★ Deliver parcels to glowing village beacons
  • ★ Recharge inside solar microgrid zones
  • ★ Avoid load-shedding (it disables charging)
  • ★ Don't drain the battery or crash
  • ★ Chain deliveries for combo multipliers
